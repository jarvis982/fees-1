Frequently Asked Questions (Fee Portal)

Clear answers to common questions about online fee payment, tax exemption, and receipts.

1. Can I use this fee receipt for Income Tax (Section 80C) deduction? +
Yes! Under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, tuition fee paid for full-time education of children is eligible for tax exemption (up to ₹1.5 Lakh per annum). Every official receipt generated through this portal clearly breaks down Tuition Fee and displays our official CBSE Affiliation Number (3530124) and Digital Seal.
2. Which UPI Apps and payment modes are supported? +
Our dynamic BharatQR scanner is compliant with NPCI standards and accepts all major UPI applications including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and mobile banking UPI apps. Parents can also choose Direct Bank Transfer (SBI NEFT/RTGS/IMPS).
3. What if money was debited from my bank but receipt wasn't generated? +
Don't worry! Your money is safe. You can simply use our "🔍 Receipt Search" tool at the top of the portal using your Admission Number or transaction UTR. Alternatively, reach our Accounts Office directly at fees@lisbhimtal.org or call +91 5942 247000.
4. Can parents pay quarterly or for multiple months together? +
Yes. In Step 2, you can select individual months or use our one-click Quarter quick-filters (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, or Select All Pending). The total amount dynamically updates in real time.
5. How can I reprint a lost fee receipt? +
You can reprint any past receipt anytime! Simply enter your student details (Admission No + DOB) to access the "Past Payment Receipts" tab, or click "Receipt Search" in the top menu and enter your Receipt Number.
